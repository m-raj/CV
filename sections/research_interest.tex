\section{\sc Ph.D. Research Summary}
My PhD research lies at the intersection of machine learning, applied mathematics, and material science. I develop data-driven and physics-informed machine-learning methods for discovering models of complex materials using computational and experimental data. The particular focus of my research is on learning governing laws, constitutive relations, and latent variables while incorporating physical structure and mathematical constraints.
